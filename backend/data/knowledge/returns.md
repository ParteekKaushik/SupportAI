### AcmeStore Knowledge Base: Global Returns Policy & Procedures

**Document ID:** KB-POL-005
**Version:** 5.1
**Classification:** Public External
**Last Updated:** March 2026 

### 1. Core Mandate and Return Rights

AcmeStore provides a structured, highly trackable operational pathway for customers seeking to return physical commodities purchased through our online portal. We strive to automate the reverse logistics lifecycle, ensuring transparency from the moment an item leaves a consumer's residence until it undergoes final diagnostic grading at our central fulfillment facilities. 

### 2. Step-by-Step Return Authorization Pipeline

No physical return will be accepted at any AcmeStore distribution hub without a valid, system-generated Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) tracking number. Unsolicited packages sent to our offices will be rejected at the loading dock or discarded without financial compensation. 

### Step 1: Digital Initiation

The customer must authenticate their credentials on the AcmeStore portal, access the "Order History" ledger, and identify the specific invoice containing the target item. The user clicks "Initiate Return" and selects a理由 (reason code) from the standardized dropdown array: 

* Code R-001: Structural item defect or mechanical malfunction.
* Code R-002: Incompatibility or incorrect sizing choice.
* Code R-003: Discrepancy between web description and physical asset.
* Code R-004: Carrier-inflicted damage during transit operations.
* Code R-005: Late delivery exceeding the guaranteed delivery window.

### Step 2: Documentation Acquisition

Upon processing the digital reason code, the system generates an RMA documentation packet comprising: 

1. **The RMA Packing Slip:** This must be placed inside the return parcel directly on top of the physical item.
2. **The Prepaid Shipping Label:** A trackable shipping manifest (via DHL, FedEx, or UPS depending on regional geography) containing the encrypted destination routing codes for the closest AcmeStore Receiving Node.

### Step 3: Package Preparation and Courier Handoff

The consumer must securely box the item, ensuring sufficient bubble wrap or structural padding protects all vulnerable surfaces. The prepaid label must be affixed to the exterior box surface, completely covering any old tracking identifiers. The parcel must be scanned into the respective carrier's network within seven (7) business days of label generation. 

### 4. Sorting, Inspection, and Grading Hierarchies

Once a returned package registers as "Delivered" at an AcmeStore logistics node, it enters our automated sorting queue. Dedicated quality control technicians inspect the asset within seventy-two (72) business hours and assign a distinct Grading Classification that dictates final processing: 

[Received Package] -> [Visual Inspection] -> [Serial Number Verification]
                                                      |
    +-----------------+-------------------------------+-----------------+

    |                 |                               |                 |
[Grade A: Pristine] [Grade B: Open-Box]     [Grade C: Refurbished]  [Grade F: Rejected]
- 100% Refundable   - 15% Fee Applied       - 30% Fee Applied       - No Refund
- Returns to Stock  - Sent to Outlet        - Sent to Repair        - Sent back to User

* **Grade A (Pristine/Factory Sealed):** The item is unopened, perfectly intact, and eligible for immediate restocking. Full financial compensation is approved.
* **Grade B (Open-Box/Excellent):** The external seal is compromised, but the item itself is immaculate with all components present. Subject to a minor restocking fee.
* **Grade C (Refurbished/Light Use):** The item shows clear operational metrics or cosmetic micro-scratches. It is sent downstream to our engineering division for reconditioning and secondary outlet sales. A partial refund is issued.
* **Grade F (Deficient/Damaged by User):** The product shows heavy unauthorized modifications, physical damage, signs of water ingress, or missing original components. The return is denied, and the product is shipped back to the consumer at their expense.

### 4. International Return Operations

Returns originating outside domestic shipping limits are subject to extended regulatory oversight. International consumers are responsible for completing all relevant Customs Declarations documents, indicating the package contents as "Returned Goods - Country of Origin: USA (or corresponding warehouse origin)." Failure to attach appropriate commercial invoices will lead to long customs delays and possible forfeiture of the shipment by border security forces. AcmeStore does not cover international customs brokers' fees or import taxes incurred during reverse logistics transport.
