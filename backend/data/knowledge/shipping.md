### 1. Operational Scope and Carrier Network

AcmeStore manages a highly complex, multi-tiered fulfillment grid designed to dispatch items rapidly across global boundaries. By integrating with premium tier-one couriers (FedEx, United Parcel Service, DHL Express, and regional postal systems), we guarantee transit visibility and robust package handling standards. 

### 2. Processing Timelines vs. Transit Timelines

A common point of confusion is the distinction between processing durations and shipping durations. 

### 2.1 Warehouse Processing Framework

All orders placed on the AcmeStore application platform require warehouse processing before being handed to a carrier. 

* **Standard Processing:** Orders submitted between Monday and Friday are processed within twenty-four to forty-eight (24–48) operational hours.
* **Weekend Exclusions:** Orders placed after 4:00 PM EST on Friday, or anytime on Saturday and Sunday, enter the processing queue the following Monday morning at 6:00 AM EST.
* **High-Volume Exceptions:** During peak holiday surges (Black Friday, Cyber Monday, End-of-Year clearances), processing phases can extend by an additional forty-eight (48) hours due to high inventory demand.

### 3. Shipping Restrictions and Geographic Exclusions

While AcmeStore targets universal delivery, infrastructural and legal barriers limit service availability in specific areas. 

### 3.1 P.O. Boxes and Military Addresses (APO/FPO/DPO)

* **P.O. Boxes:** Only Standard Ground shipping tiers can deliver to United States Postal Service P.O. Boxes. Expedited and Priority tiers utilize private carriers who cannot legally access federal post box receptacles.
* **Military Logistics:** Shipments directed to Army Post Office (APO), Fleet Post Office (FPO), and Diplomatic Post Office (DPO) hubs travel via the military postal system. Transit durations can take up to forty-five (45) calendar days. Tracking accuracy drops once the package transitions from civilian to military sorting networks.

### 3.2 Hazardous Materials (HazMat) Regulations

Products containing lithium-ion batteries, volatile chemical compounds, aerosol containers, or strong adhesives are subject to stringent Department of Transportation (DOT) and Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) rules. These items must travel exclusively via surface-based Ground Transport. Air-based Next-Day Priority shipping options are blocked during checkout when these items are present in the shopping cart. 

### 4. Delivery Anomalies, Lost Shipments, and Risk Mitigation

### 4.1 Tracking Verification and "Porch Piracy"

When a carrier updates a tracking link to "Delivered," AcmeStore legally considers its basic delivery contract fulfilled. If a customer cannot find a package marked as delivered, they must execute the following protocol: 

1. Examine secondary entrance areas, patios, back porches, and leasing offices.
2. Confirm with household members or adjacent neighbors who may have accepted the parcel.
3. Wait an additional twenty-four (24) hours; carriers occasionally trigger delivery updates early while still in transit.

### 4.2 Initiating a Lost Package Investigation

If the parcel remains missing after forty-eight (48) hours from the tracking update, the user must contact AcmeStore Support to initiate a formal trace. AcmeStore will open an official investigation claim with the respective courier. This review process takes five (5) to seven (7) business days. During this period, the carrier attempts to recover GPS coordinates from the delivery scan to verify the exact drop-off location. If the carrier confirms a misdelivery, AcmeStore will dispatch a replacement order or issue a full refund.
