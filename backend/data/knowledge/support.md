### 1. Omnichannel Engagement Framework

The AcmeStore Customer Support division provides technical troubleshooting, order management, account recovery, and transactional assistance across multiple communication channels. We aim to offer consistent support across all channels, driven by clear Service Level Agreements (SLAs) that respect our users' time. 

### 2. Support Channels and Operating Hours

Customers can reach out to our customer care teams through four primary channels: 

### 2.1 Live Chat and Conversational AI

* **Availability:** 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year.
* **First-Response SLA:** Less than two (2) minutes for connection to a live agent following initial automated screening.
* **Best Used For:** Immediate tracking status checks, address updates, rapid order cancellations, and clear promotional validation.

### 2.2 Telephonic Voice Support

* **Availability:** Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern Standard Time (EST).
* **First-Response SLA:** Average queue hold time under eight (8) minutes during standard operating windows.
* **Contact Number:** 1-800-555-ACME (1-800-555-2263).
* **Best Used For:** High-value billing discrepancies, complex multi-item return coordinations, and escalating unresolved digital issues.

### 2.3 Electronic Mail (Email Helpdesk)

* **Availability:** Inbound ticketing accepted continuously.
* **First-Response SLA:** Within twelve (12) business hours from receipt of the inbound message.
* **Support Email Address:** support@acmestore.com.
* **Best Used For:** Submitting photographic proof of product defects, sending formal corporate documentation, and maintaining a written record of long conversations.

* **Tier 1 (Critical System Failure / Security Breaches):** Compromised customer accounts, unauthorized financial charges, potential data leaks, or complete checkout system outages. Immediate escalation to engineering and security teams occurs.
* **Tier 2 (Transactional & Logistical Complications):** Orders displaying tracking anomalies, requests to change shipping addresses before fulfillment, invalid coupon applications on high-value checkouts, or damaged items received by customers.
* **Tier 3 (General Informational Inquiries):** Standard questions regarding restock dates, product sizing adjustments, generic feature clarifications, or basic interpretations of corporate policy.

### 4. De-escalation Policies and Code of Conduct

AcmeStore support staff are trained to handle challenging situations with empathy, precision, and respect. However, we maintain strict safety policies to protect our employees. Support agents are authorized to terminate live chat sessions or phone calls if a customer uses profane language, makes personal threats, or displays discriminatory behavior. The agent will issue one formal warning. If the behavior continues, the agent will disconnect the session and forward the interaction logs to our Corporate Legal and Security division for review, which may result in a permanent account ban. 

### 5. Escalating Unresolved Disputes

If a standard customer support agent cannot resolve an issue, the customer can request a "Tier 2 Supervisor Review." 

1. The frontline agent transfers the ticket tracking log to an active supervisor queue.
2. A manager reviews the entire history of the ticket within twenty-four (24) business hours.
3. The supervisor contacts the customer directly with a final resolution offer.
4. If this step fails, disputes move to formal binding arbitration, bypassed only for small claims court matters within the customer's home state jurisdiction.
